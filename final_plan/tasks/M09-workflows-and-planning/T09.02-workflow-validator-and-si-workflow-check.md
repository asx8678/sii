### Goal
Reject workflows that can't run correctly before they run, with precise messages.

### Deliverables
- `lib/si/workflows/validator.ex` (`Si.Workflows.Validator`); CLI `si workflow check <name|file>`; tests `test/si/workflows/validator_test.exs`.

### Specification
Rules (each error names the step or role and the rule):
1. `needs` refer to existing steps; the step graph is acyclic (`Graph.is_acyclic?/1` with libgraph).
2. Every role referenced by `by`, `spawn`, `review.by`, `critique.by` exists.
3. Writer roles (`spawn` of build steps) have `worktree: true`; reviewer roles do not.
4. Every `review.by` role differs from its step's writer role and, using the first preferred model of each and `Si.Models.diversity/2`, is `other_family`; `same_family` is a warning, `same_model_fresh` an error.
5. `loop` steps have `max_rounds` between 1 and 10; `until` parses.
6. `gate` values are valid; `deliver.target` valid; `pr` requires `gate: you`.
7. `budget.total` parses (`"$6"`, `"6"`, `6.0`) and is > 0; `supervision_share` in (0, 0.5].
8. Tier consistency: `full` requires a plan gate `you`.
`si workflow check` prints `OK <hash>` or the list of errors and warnings; exit 0 without errors (warnings allowed), 1 otherwise. (`--dry-run` is added in T09.03.)

### Tests
One failing fixture per rule; all templates pass; warnings do not fail.

### Acceptance criteria
Tests pass; `bin/ci` passes.

### Verify

**Expected result.** `si workflow check` rejects workflows that violate any of the eight rules. Each message names the step or role and the rule. Warnings do not fail the check.

**Checks**
1. One failing fixture per rule.
   Expected: exactly one error, naming that rule and the step or role.
2. All the templates.
   Expected: `OK <hash>` and exit 0.
3. A `same_family` reviewer.
   Expected: a warning, and exit 0. A `same_model_fresh` reviewer gives an error and exit 1.

**Redo if**
- Validation stops at the first error. Rewrite it to report all errors in one pass.
