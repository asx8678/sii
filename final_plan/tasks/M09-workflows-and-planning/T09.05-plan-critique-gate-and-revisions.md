### Goal
Another model critiques every plan; the gate depends on the tier; each re-plan is a new revision that says what it keeps, changes or cancels.

### Deliverables
- `priv/prompts/critic/plan_review.md`; critique and gate logic in the plan step; `Si.Plan.diff_revisions/2`; tests.

### Specification
- Critic: role `critic`, profile `reviewer`, model = `Si.Models.pick_reviewer(lead_model, [models.critic.model | models.critic.alternatives])`; context = goal + acceptance + the plan as a readable list + the repo's risky paths; the critic uses `si_verdict` with a review of `purpose: :plan` on a thread with `subject_kind: "plan"` (add this subject kind).
- Revise → restart a Lead session with the critique as input (the plan prompt plus `Critique: <findings>`), new `si_plan` → revision `n + 1` (previous `superseded`); at most 2 critique rounds.
- Gate: `auto` (quick; plans don't exist there), `lead` (standard): approve when the critic approved; otherwise after 2 rounds escalate to you; `you` (full): inbox item `kind: "plan_gate"` with the plan as Markdown, the critique and options `["approve", "revise", "cancel"]` (`revise` requires a note, which goes to the Lead as input).
- Approval → revision `approved`; the build step reads the approved revision.
- `diff_revisions(old, new)`: steps matched by id; `kept` (same content hash), `changed`, `added`, `cancelled`; stored in `plan_revisions.changes`. Applying a revision during execution (used by T12.04): cancel tasks of cancelled steps (if not landed), update tasks of changed steps that have not started, create tasks for added steps.

### Tests
Critic approve → Lead gate passes; critic revise twice → escalation; full tier creates a plan_gate item and `revise` with a note restarts planning; revision diff cases.

### Acceptance criteria

- Gates follow the tier: `lead` or `you`.
- Each re-plan is a new revision, with a kept, changed, added and cancelled diff.

**Checks**

   Expected: an `other_family` model, or the documented fallback, flagged.
2. The critic revises twice.
   Expected: escalation to the owner.
3. The `full` tier.
   Expected: a `plan_gate` inbox item; `revise` requires a note and restarts planning with it.
4. `diff_revisions/2` on the fixtures.
   Expected: exact `kept`, `changed`, `added` and `cancelled` lists.

**Redo if**
