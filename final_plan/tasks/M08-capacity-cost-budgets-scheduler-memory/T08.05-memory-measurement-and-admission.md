### Goal
Start new sessions only when the machine has memory for them, based on measured memory per role.

### Deliverables
- `lib/si/memory.ex` (`Si.Memory`, sampler GenServer); settings `memory.budget_mb` (0 means auto = 60% of physical RAM) and `workers.max_cap` (default 8); tests `test/si/memory_test.exs`.

### Specification
```elixir
@spec can_start?(String.t()) :: boolean()          # role
@spec estimate(String.t()) :: pos_integer()        # MB
@spec snapshot() :: %{total_mb: integer(), budget_mb: integer(), by_session: map(), by_role: map()}
@spec max_workers() :: pos_integer()
```
- Every 10 s: for each live Si session, RSS of its OS process plus descendants (`Si.OsProcess.rss_kb/1`, `descendants/1`).
- Per-role estimate: rolling average of sessions older than 2 minutes; defaults before measurements: builder 1200 MB, astra 400 MB, lead 800 MB, critic/arbiter 400 MB.
- Physical RAM: `sysctl -n hw.memsize` on macOS, `/proc/meminfo` MemTotal on Linux.
- `can_start?/1`: current total + estimate ≤ budget.
- `max_workers/0` = `min(workers.max_cap, div(budget, estimate("builder")))`, at least 1. The dispatcher and review runner check `can_start?/1` before starting sessions and wait otherwise (event once per wait).
- The sampler reads through an injectable function so tests don't depend on real processes.

### Tests
Estimates switch from defaults to measurements; `can_start?/1` thresholds; `max_workers/0` math; auto budget calculation from a fake RAM value.

### Acceptance criteria

**Expected result.**
- Memory per session (RSS of the process and its descendants) is sampled every 10 s.
- Estimates per role become measured averages.
- `can_start?/1` and `max_workers/0` follow the budget: by default, 60% of physical RAM.
- The sampler is injectable.

**Checks**
1. `sysctl -n hw.memsize`, then `Si.Memory.snapshot()` with an automatic budget.
   Expected: `budget_mb` is 60% of that value.
2. Estimates before and after measurements (a fake sampler).
   Expected: defaults at first, then the rolling average of sessions older than 2 minutes.
3. The `max_workers/0` math.
   Expected: `min(workers.max_cap, div(budget, estimate("builder")))`, and at least 1.

**Redo if**
- Tests depend on real processes' memory. Rewrite them with the injectable sampler.
