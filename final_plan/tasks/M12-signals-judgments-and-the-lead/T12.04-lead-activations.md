### Goal
Short, stateless Lead decisions from the ledger when the ladder escalates, a worker asks a question, or a scope request needs a decision.

### Deliverables
- Migration `create_lead_activations`; `protocol/lead_decision.schema.json`; `priv/prompts/lead/decide.md`; `lib/si/lead.ex` (`Si.Lead`); tests with `Req.Test` stubs.

### Specification
```elixir
@spec activate(%{kind: :signal | :question | :scope | :replan, task_id: String.t() | nil, run_id: String.t() | nil, ref: String.t()}) :: {:ok, map()} | {:error, term()}
```
- Checks first: not frozen (`Si.Freeze`), supervision budget available; otherwise escalate to you.
- Context (≤ 8,000 characters): the task ledger summary (T06.08), open signals with evidence, interventions so far and their outcomes, the question or scope request, the plan revision summary.
- One direct model call (ReqLLM, `models.lead`) with a structured output validated against `lead_decision.schema.json`: `{action: note | hint | steer | reassign | replan | escalate | answer | approve_scope | refuse_scope, message, rationale, model (for reassign)}`.
- Execute: `hint`/`steer` → deliver; `reassign` → stop the worker, interrupt the attempt, start a new attempt with `model` (validated against the diversity rule) and the message as a summary of what failed; `replan` → a new plan revision (T09.05 flow); `answer` → resolve the `si_ask` inbox item with the answer and deliver it; scope decisions → resolve the request; `escalate` → inbox. Record `lead_activations` (trigger, decision, model, cost) and event `lead.decided`.
- Wire-up: the ladder's Lead rungs, `si_ask` (the Lead answers when confident; its decision may be `escalate` instead), and scope requests that were not auto-approved.

### Tests
Each action with a stubbed decision; invalid decision → escalate; frozen or out of budget → inbox without a model call.

**Expected result.**
- Short, stateless Lead decisions come from the ledger: one model call with a validated structured output.
- They are executed, recorded, and wired into the ladder, `si_ask` and scope requests.
- When Si is frozen or the budget is used up, the question goes to the owner without a model call.

**Checks**
1. Each action with a stubbed decision.
   Expected: the right effect, and a `lead_activations` row.
2. An invalid decision.
   Expected: `escalate`.
3. Frozen, or out of budget.
   Expected: an inbox item, and the `Req.Test` stub sees no request.
4. `reassign` to a model that breaks diversity.
   Expected: refused, and escalated.

**Redo if**
- The Lead keeps a long-lived chat session between activations. Rewrite it: each activation is a single call built from the ledger.
