### Goal
A writer can dispute a finding with evidence; a third model from another family decides; you decide when it is still unclear.

### Deliverables
- The `si_dispute` handler; `priv/prompts/arbiter/dispute.md`; `lib/si/review/dispute.ex`; tests (`@tag :pi`).

### Specification
- `si_dispute {finding_id, argument, evidence}`: the finding must be open and belong to the writer's task thread; set it `disputed`; reply `%{accepted: true, pending: true}`.
- Arbiter: model from `Si.Models.pick_reviewer/2` excluding both the writer's and Astra's families (fallback: a different model than both); role `arbiter`, profile `reviewer`; context: the finding (problem, evidence), the writer's argument and evidence, the relevant diff hunks; the arbiter uses `si_verdict` with `purpose: :dispute`: `approve` = the writer is right → finding `dismissed`; `revise` = the finding stands → back to `open`. A summary containing `UNCLEAR` (instructed in the prompt) → inbox item for you with both sides.
- The outcome is delivered to the writer as a follow-up and recorded (event `finding.status_changed`).
- Reviewer precision (T13.02) counts dismissed findings against the reviewer.

### Tests
Dismiss, uphold and unclear paths with faux arbiters; disputing a non-open finding is rejected.

### Acceptance criteria
Tests pass; `bin/ci` passes.

**Expected result.** A writer can dispute a finding with evidence. An arbiter from a third family decides; an `UNCLEAR` summary goes to the owner. The outcome reaches the writer and counts in the reviewer's precision.

**Checks**
1. The arbiter's family.
   Expected: different from both the writer's and Astra's.
2. A dispute of a finding that is not open.
   Expected: rejected.
3. The dismiss, uphold and unclear paths.
   Expected: the finding's status changes accordingly, and the writer gets a follow-up.

**Redo if**
- Astra judges disputes of her own findings. Rewrite it to use an arbiter session.
