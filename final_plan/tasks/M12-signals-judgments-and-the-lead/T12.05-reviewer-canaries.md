### Goal
Measure how many real bugs each reviewer model catches by planting a known bug in a copy of a change in a small share of reviews.

### Deliverables
- Migration `create_canaries`; `lib/si/review/canary.ex` (`Si.Review.Canary`) with simple mutators; tests (`@tag :pi` for the blind review).

### Specification
- Trigger: after an approve verdict, with probability `review.canary_rate` (default 0.05), for changes with at least one changed source line.
- Mutation on a random changed line (seeded RNG recorded on the canary): TS/JS: `<`↔`<=`, `>`↔`>=`, `===`↔`!==`, `&&`↔`||`, a numeric literal ±1, remove a `!`; Elixir: `==`↔`!=`, `<`↔`<=`, `and`↔`or`, a numeric literal ±1. Skip lines where no mutator applies; give up after 20 tries.
- Apply the mutation in a temporary `:astra`-purpose worktree at the approved snapshot, freeze it (base = the task base) as the canary snapshot.
- Blind review: a new session with the same reviewer model, a normal review context (nothing mentions the canary), `purpose: :canary`, its own thread (`subject_kind: "canary"`), never linked to the task.
- `caught` when any blocking finding names the mutated file with a line within ±3 of the mutation. Record the canary; the result feeds the scoreboard (T13.02) and the targets (T07.04). Canary snapshots never reach a writer or the merge queue.
- Cost counts as supervision.

### Tests
Mutator unit tests per language; `@tag :pi` faux reviewer that flags the line (caught) and one that approves (missed).

### Acceptance criteria
Tests pass; `bin/ci` passes.

**Expected result.**
- After a small share of approvals, a mutated copy of the change is reviewed blind by the same reviewer model.
- A canary counts as caught when a blocking finding is within ±3 lines of the mutation.
- Canaries never reach writers or the merge queue.

**Checks**
1. The blind context.
   Expected: nothing in it mentions a canary; it has its own thread (`subject_kind: "canary"`).
2. The canary snapshot.
   Expected: never enqueued, and never linked to the task.
3. The mutators, per language (tests).
   Expected: every listed swap is applied correctly; after 20 failed tries the canary gives up.

**Redo if**
- The canary review shares a thread or a session with the real review. Rewrite it to be separate.
