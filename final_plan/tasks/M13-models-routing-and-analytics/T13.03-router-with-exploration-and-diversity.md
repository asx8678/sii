### Goal
Pick the model for each role from measured results, within budget and capacity, never breaking the diversity rule, with a small exploration share.

### Deliverables
`lib/si/models/router.ex`; settings `router.exploration` (default 0.05) and role weights; the dispatcher, review runner and Lead use it; tests (PropCheck for diversity).

### Specification
```elixir
@spec pick(String.t(), map()) :: {:ok, String.t(), %{reason: atom(), expected_cost: float(), counterfactual: String.t() | nil}}
#   context: counterpart_model (writer or reviewer), budget_remaining, tier, risky (boolean), pinned (model | nil)
```
- Candidates: the role's preferred list plus its ladder; drop those failing diversity against the counterpart (reviewers must be `other_family`; builders must keep their reviewer `other_family`), those whose expected cost exceeds the remaining budget, and those whose pool queue is longer than 20.
- Score: weighted sum of normalized metrics per role (builder: pass rate 0.4, rounds 0.2, cost 0.2, latency 0.2; reviewer: precision 0.4, canary catch 0.4, cost 0.2); missing metrics use the candidate's rank in the preferred list.
- Exploration: with probability `router.exploration` pick uniformly among the other eligible candidates, except for `full` tier or risky changes.
- `pinned` model: use it, but compute and return the counterfactual pick and its expected cost (shown in the cockpit).

### Tests
Selection with fixture scores; exploration frequency (seeded, 10,000 picks ≈ 5%); PropCheck: the picked reviewer is always `other_family` relative to the counterpart when any eligible candidate exists.

### Acceptance criteria
Tests pass; `bin/ci` passes.

**Expected result.**
- The router picks models per role from measured scores, within budget and capacity, and never breaks diversity.
- It explores about 5% of the time, but not for the full tier or risky changes.
- For pinned models, it reports what it would have picked instead.

**Checks**
1. 10,000 seeded picks.
   Expected: about 5% exploration (within ±1%).
2. The PropCheck property.
   Expected: the picked reviewer is always `other_family` whenever an eligible candidate exists.
3. The `full` tier.
   Expected: no exploration.

**Redo if**
- Diversity is checked after scoring and the pick is then dropped without falling back. Filter the candidates first, then score them.
